What is Kybermittari?
Kybermittari (Cybermeter) is a free national cybersecurity assessment framework from the Finnish National Cyber Security Centre (NCSC-FI) at Traficom. Organisations use it to assess and improve their cybersecurity and to compare themselves with others. It is mostly based on the NIST Cybersecurity Framework and the U.S. DOE's C2M2 model


- Assessment process: Management starts the assessment and chooses who leads it. Then the team prepares, fills in the tool, plans improvements, and repeats the check later.
- Maturity model: There are 11 domains, like risk management and access control, and 4 levels (0–3). You answer practices, and a level is reached when more than half of its practices are done.
- Maturity report: It shows the level of every domain and objective, and what to improve next.
- Spreadsheet: This is the actual tool. You answer questions on 11 domain tabs, and it makes the reports and charts automatically
